Literature Review

Paper notes, key takeaways, and useful repositories.

RT-2: Vision-Language-Action Models Transfer Web Knowledge to Robotic Control

Brohan, A. et al. (Google DeepMind, 2023) 📄 arXiv
My Notes

RT-2 co-fine-tunes a large vision-language model (VLM) on robotics data so that the model's web-scale semantic understanding can be leveraged for manipulation. Key insight: representing robot actions as text tokens lets the model generalize to novel tasks zero-shot. Highly relevant to integrating language-conditioned control for patient-transfer tasks.

4D-Humans: Reconstructing and Tracking Humans with Transformers

Goel, S. et al. (UC Berkeley, 2023) 📄 arXiv
My Notes

Proposes a transformer-based approach for Human Mesh Recovery (HMR) that works across video sequences (4D = 3D + time). Strong temporal consistency without per-video optimization. Directly applicable to estimating patient body pose in real-time for safe robot-patient contact planning.
