Hello, I'm David! 👋

I am a Robotics Research Engineer and PhD student working at the intersection of industry and academia. My primary research focuses on patient transfer robots — systems designed to safely and autonomously assist with patient handling in clinical and home environments.

My work bridges the gap between theoretical research and practical deployment, with a strong emphasis on embodied AI, human-robot interaction, robotic grasping, motion planning, and robust control systems.

I am particularly interested in how Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models and Human Mesh Recovery (HMR) techniques can improve robotic perception and safety in dynamic, unstructured environments. My toolchain includes ROS2, MoveIt2, PyTorch, and MuJoCo.
