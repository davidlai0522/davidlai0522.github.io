00 ABOUT

I'm David, a Robotics Specialist at Schaeffler in Singapore and a part-time PhD student in Robotics at Nanyang Technological University (NTU). I build the software that lets robots see, grasp, move and learn: perception, grasping with parallel grippers and dexterous hands, mobile robots, humanoids and robot learning.

My research focuses on human digital twins, physical human-robot interaction and machine intelligence: helping robots understand people and work safely alongside them. Before that I earned a Bachelor's in Mechanical Engineering at NTU with Highest Distinction, specialising in robotics and mechatronics.

01 VISION & MISSION

"My dream is to unleash the potential of robotics technology to enhance human life."

Digital models of people (their motion, behaviour and physical state) so robots can predict and adapt to the humans they work with.

Physical human-robot interaction

Robots that work hands-on with people: sharing loads, guiding motion and reacting safely to contact forces.

Machine intelligence

Giving robots the ability to perceive, learn and decide, so they can handle new situations instead of only scripted ones.
